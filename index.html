Put it on your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. If you see an Open as Web App switch, leave it on. Tap Add.
  5. Open it from the new icon, then tap Notifications to turn on alerts.

Notifications on iPhone work only from the Home Screen icon, not from a Safari tab.
